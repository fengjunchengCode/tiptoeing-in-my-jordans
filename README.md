# Tiptoeing in My Jordans

Notes and free prompts for the "tiptoeing in my jordans" meme: the AI clip of a grinning man in a velvet tailcoat who sneaks into a straw-covered barn, shushes the camera and tiptoes across the floor. It is also called the Rumpelstiltskin tiptoe meme.

## Where the meme comes from

- According to Know Your Meme, the original AI video was posted on Instagram on August 27, 2026.
- It took off on TikTok at the end of September 2026, when users paired it with RiFF RAFF's song "Tip Toe Wing In My Jawwdinz".
- It is not a scene from the 1987 Rumpelstiltskin movie, and the song is not part of the original clip.

## Make one with your own photo

[Tiptoeing in My Jordans](https://tiptoeing-in-my-jordans.com/) turns one clear photo of a person or a pet into a 10-second video of the routine.

- About 5 minutes per video

## Tips for a good result

- Use one sharp, front-facing photo with the whole face visible.
- Add the trending sound inside TikTok, Reels or Shorts when you post. The song is copyrighted, so it is not baked into the video.
